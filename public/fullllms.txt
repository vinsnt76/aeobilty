# 2. Core Technical Concepts & Definitions

1. **Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)**: The discipline of refactoring copy, headers, and schema so Large Language Models extract standalone context passages without ambiguity.
2. **Geographic Engine Optimisation (GEO)**: Optimising location proximity signals, coordinate precision, NAP consistency, and local map pack salience for AI voice search.
3. **Atomic Answer Blocks**: Direct 90-120 token definition blocks positioned immediately beneath natural-language question headings to capture maximum positional attention weights in RAG scrapers.
4. **Positional Bias**: The mathematical phenomenon where LLM attention mechanisms prioritize tokens at the head and tail of document chunks over middle context.
5. **Entity Salience**: The vector similarity score measuring how confidently a generative model connects a brand node to a specific topic or service intent.
6. **FAQ Knowledge Graph**: A structured multi-entity topology that maps parent root questions, child implementation nodes, and sibling radial trust loops into deterministic `@graph` schema markup.
